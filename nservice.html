What nServices app can do?

nServices App Software is a SaaS solution for Companies or individuals who want to run a Home or Local Services Business. It’s a complete white-label app. You can customize it as your own brand with its color scheme, features etc.

  • 🔄Aggregator (Multiple Services & Providers)
    You run your own business like Swiggy or Uber. Add different Service Providers under categories, manage payments, assign jobs, take commissions. Consumers book the service from service providers. You act as an intermediary.
  • 🧍Single Service (Independent Service Providers)
    You run your own business for single services like food delivery, electricians, plumbers, spa, salon, AC repair. Customer order food from you directly. You act as service provider.
  • 🏢App For Your Business
    You can use nServices app to grow your existing business or start a new one. Manage all your services and service providers under one brand. The system is white-label. Customers will find it easy to book services, schedule, and pay online.
